2. Description of the Service

Zero | X is designed to help Discord server owners and administrators detect, respond to, and mitigate potentially harmful or suspicious activity.

The Bot may provide the following security features:

  • Anti-Raid Detection — Detects unusually large numbers of server joins occurring within a short period of time.
  • Anti-Bot Protection — Identifies and flags potentially suspicious or unauthorized bot accounts.
  • Anti-Nuke Protection — Detects potentially malicious mass creation, deletion, or modification of Discord channels and roles.
  • Anti-Spam Protection — Detects excessive or repetitive message activity.
  • Mass Mention Protection — Detects potentially abusive use of @everyone and @here mentions.
  • Automatic Lockdown — May temporarily restrict certain server activity when suspicious behavior is detected.
  • Security Alerts — May send security-related events, detections, and actions to a designated private security or logging channel.
  • Whitelist / Trusted Users — Allows authorized server administrators to designate trusted users who may perform sensitive actions without triggering certain protections.
  • Emergency Lockdown — Provides an administrator-controlled command, such as /lockdown, intended to quickly restrict server activity during a security incident.

Features may be added, removed, modified, or improved over time.
